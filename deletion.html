Data Deletion

The publishing tools store no data about anyone other than their owner. If you connected an account and want it removed, revoke the app in your account settings (Threads/Instagram: Settings → Account → Website permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Stored tokens are deleted within 7 days of a request sent by Instagram DM to @he110w0r1.oi.
